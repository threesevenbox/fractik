PRIVACY POLICY - FRACTIK

Effective date: September 13, 2026

1. INTRODUCTION

Welcome to FRACTIK. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we handle data when you play our game.

2. DATA WE COLLECT

We collect minimal data necessary to ensure smooth gameplay and fix technical errors:

  • Technical and diagnostic data: Crash reports, device model, and OS version.
  • Gameplay data: Anonymous player ID, progress, and performance scores.

3. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA

The collected data is used exclusively for maintaining and improving game performance, as well as analyzing and fixing crashes or bugs.

4. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

We may use third-party service providers (such as Firebase / Google Analytics for Firebase) to collect diagnostic logs and app stability metrics.

5. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

We store crash logs and diagnostic data for a maximum of 90 days, after which they are automatically purged or deleted.

6. YOUR RIGHTS AND DATA DELETION REQUEST

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of your data at any time.

To submit a request for data deletion or access, please fill out our dedicated form: Data Deletion Request Form
